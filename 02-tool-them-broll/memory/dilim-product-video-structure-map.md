## Common script structure across DiLiM product-pitch videos (confirmed across 6 reference videos, 3 products)

Every product-pitch video analyzed so far follows the same rough skeleton, regardless of product:
1. **Hook** — an alarming/surprising fact or contrast device (something recoverable vs. something not; "you're only 30 but living with a 60-year-old's X").
2. **Symptom list, read in rapid succession** — a cluster of related symptoms named back-to-back in one breath (e.g. "đau đầu, vai gáy, chóng mặt, mất ngủ, tê bì tay chân, rối loạn tiền đình"). This exact cluster recurs verbatim across DIFFERENT products (Nano Sụn, Rich Coenzyme Q10, Raydel Policosanol all use it) — it's a stock symptom-list, not product-specific.
3. **Root-cause explanation** — why this happens mechanistically (aging, energy depletion, mechanical wear, etc.), often via a metaphor (rust/rỉ sét, worn shoes, car engine oil, electricity/mansion).
4. **Product introduction** — brand, country of origin, manufacturing standard (GMP Nhật Bản), clinical studies count, exclusivity ("phân phối độc quyền").
5. **Key ingredient spotlight** — one or two named actives get individual attention (Astaxanthin recurs across Rich Coenzyme Q10 AND Raydel combo videos — chống oxy hóa mạnh gấp 6000 lần vitamin C, verbatim across products).
6. **Mechanism/benefit explained with an analogy** — LDL/HDL "dọn dẹp" metaphor, Coenzyme Q10 as "nhà máy điện"/electricity for a mansion or luxury car, flagship-tier comparison (Mercedes Maybach, iPhone Pro Max) for premium product lines.
7. **Dosage/regimen specifics** — real self-demo footage of presenter holding actual pills/bottles, explaining exact dose and timing.
8. **Closing CTA** — "để lại tên/SĐT hoặc gọi hotline", mandatory disclaimer "sản phẩm này không phải là thuốc và không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh".

**Key insight**: near-identical script structure is reused across different recordings of the SAME product (the 2 Rich Coenzyme Q10 videos, the 2 Raydel videos) — but B-roll clip CHOICES are NOT copy-pasted between them; each recording pulls different real clips for the same idea. Confirms rule 13 (no-repeat) is achievable because the library is deep enough, and matches the house style even in the user's own reference edits.

## B-roll visual style is NOT one fixed template — at least 4 distinct compositing styles observed across reference videos, used situationally

1. **Hard-edge rectangular box overlay** (top ~55% of frame, opaque) — seen in "Sụn khớp/Phân Tích Sụn" analysis videos.
2. **Soft-edge semi-transparent full/near-full-frame blend** (40-70% opacity, feathered) — seen in Rich Coenzyme Q10 video 1 ("Tế bào não tổn thương").
3. **Floating glowing cutout portrait** (person silhouette with glow/light-leak edge, floats over background rather than a hard box) — seen in Rich Coenzyme Q10 video 2 ("30 tuổi... cái đầu 60").
4. **Small floating PiP inset** (rounded-rect window, notably smaller than full width, layered mid-frame) — seen in Raydel Policosanol videos (car-driving metaphor shot, stroke-collapse shot).
5. **Hard vertical split-screen** (left/right halves) — seen once for the "car engine oil" metaphor in Rich Coenzyme Q10 video 1.

Plus **non-visual-overlay B-roll types**: real product self-demo (presenter holding actual pills/boxes, not inserted footage), zoomed-in crops of the product's own printed packaging graphics (e.g. "-LDL/HDL+" icon on the Raydel box), and screenshots of clinical research charts/graphs used as "proof" B-roll.

**Implication**: our own pipeline (`render_overlay.py`) currently only implements style #1 (hard top-band crop, `BAND_H=672`). The user's own reference material uses a much wider repertoire. Don't assume the top-band style is the only "correct" DiLiM look — it was simply what got built first and validated on 3 non-product test videos. When the user next asks to build a product-pitch video, ask/confirm whether to keep the current top-band engine or extend it toward one of these other styles, rather than assuming.

## Two distinct copywriting "voices" observed — match the right one when building captions for a given product

**Voice A — direct/enthusiastic, feature-list heavy.** Used in: Ellagic Acid combo (all 6), Raydel Policosanol (both), original Rich Coenzyme Q10 (both), Rich+DHA 12-tháng. Characterized by: short declarative sentences, explicit feature call-outs ("đây là...", "thứ nhất... thứ hai... thứ ba..."), superlative claims stated plainly (bán chạy số 1, gấp 6000 lần, độ tinh khiết 99%), direct address energy ("anh chị nha" as a verbal tic almost every sentence).

**Voice B — narrative/philosophical, metaphor-driven.** Used in: all 5 of the Nano Nattokinase + Rich Coenzyme Q10 combo videos ("ngủ đủ nhưng vẫn đau đầu", "Chóng mặt lảo đảo", "LẦN CUỐI CÙNG...", "SAU TUỔI 40...", "VÌ SAO BỊ ĐỘT QUỴ"). Characterized by: a reflective opening question aimed at the viewer's own self-honesty ("lần cuối cùng bạn thực sự nghĩ đến sức khỏe của mình là khi nào"), an extended metaphor developed over several sentences rather than a one-liner (aging blood vessels = a road accumulating small cracks over a year; a canal silting up when the flow slows; a "kiềng ba chân"/3-legged-stool framing for "3 trục nền tảng"), explicit reassurance that symptoms are "không phải là bệnh" but "tín hiệu/lời nhắc", and a consistent "2 gốc rễ" (two roots) framing: (1) mạch máu phải thông/sạch, (2) tế bào phải đủ năng lượng — mapped directly onto Nattokinase (root 1) and Rich Coenzyme Q10 dạng khử (root 2). Much lower feature-list density; relies on repetition of the SAME two-root logic across all 5 videos almost verbatim in places.

**How to apply**: before writing captions for a new product-pitch video, check which voice the source A-roll script uses (read the actual transcript tone, don't assume by product) and mirror it — a Voice-B script rendered with Voice-A's punchy list-style captions (or vice versa) would clash with the spoken pacing and undercut the intended emotional arc.

## Symptom-cluster → folder cheat sheet (recurs across products)

The stock symptom list "đau đầu, vai gáy, chóng mặt, mất ngủ, tê bì tay chân, rối loạn tiền đình, hay quên, kém tập trung" appears verbatim across Nano Sụn, Rich Coenzyme Q10, and Raydel videos even though the ROOT CAUSE framing differs per product (joint wear vs. brain energy vs. blood lipids). When this exact cluster appears, pull from: `Đau đầu - Chóng mặt - Mệt mõi - Bệnh`, `Ngủ- Ngon- mất ngủ`, `Đã Chuẩn Hóa` (hay quên/trí nhớ), and treat each named symptom as its own caption+B-roll beat (rule from [[dilim-broll-selection-principles]] about not gộm nhiều ý vào 1 caption).
