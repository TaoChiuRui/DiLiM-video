**Mục đích cốt lõi (2026-07-17, user nhấn mạnh "ghi nhớ thật kỹ"):** B-roll tồn tại để DIỄN TẢ lời nói trong A-roll, giúp người xem dễ hình dung/dễ hiểu. Một video dài chứa rất nhiều ý (idea) khác nhau — mỗi ý cần đúng 1 video minh họa riêng. Đây là gốc rễ của mọi quy tắc chọn B-roll bên dưới: không phải "tìm clip gắn nhãn khớp từ khóa" một cách máy móc, mà là hỏi "clip này có giúp người xem HÌNH DUNG ĐƯỢC điều đang nói không?" trước khi chọn.

**Nhịp dựng (2026-07-21):** ngoài việc minh họa, B-roll/text còn có mục đích thứ 2 quan trọng không kém — giữ nhịp, duy trì sự chú ý của người xem. Cứ vài giây nên có 1 ý nhỏ xuất hiện (chữ, B-roll, hoặc cả 2), vì nếu để 1 đoạn dài không có gì minh họa, video sẽ nhàm chán dù nội dung A-roll vẫn đang nói liên tục. Hệ quả thực tế: khi cân nhắc "để trống vì không có match tốt" (xem mục 2), phải tự hỏi thêm "để trống đoạn này có làm nhịp bị đứt quá lâu không" trước khi chấp nhận — nếu có, tìm kỹ hơn (kể cả hình minh họa mang tính khái niệm/hỗ trợ, không cần khớp chính xác tuyệt đối) thay vì bỏ cuộc sau 1 lần tìm không ra. Sau khi dựng xong 1 bản nháp, nên tự quét lại toàn bộ timeline tìm những đoạn liên tục nhiều giây không có cả chữ lẫn B-roll — đó là dấu hiệu đáng nghi cần xem lại. Nhịp đến từ mật độ Ý THẬT trong lời nói được bám sát đúng, không phải từ cắt cảnh trang trí cho vui (xem thêm phần "misread" ở mục 1 bên dưới — 2 điều này liên quan chặt: cắt nhanh trong video mẫu là do ý dày đặc thật, và bám đúng ý dày đặc đó chính là thứ tạo ra nhịp).

**Khi ý trừu tượng không có clip khớp — dùng TEXT (có animation) làm công cụ giữ nhịp thay B-roll (2026-07-21):** nhiều video có ý lan man/trừu tượng, không có đối tượng cụ thể nào trong thư viện để chèn. Trong trường hợp đó, KHÔNG bỏ qua luôn cả đoạn (không caption, không B-roll) — vẫn giữ caption có animation, vì bản thân chữ CHUYỂN ĐỘNG đã đủ giữ nhịp/thu hút chú ý (con người tự nhiên bị hút mắt bởi chuyển động trong 1 khung hình tĩnh, VD người nói ngồi yên). Tức là "để trống nếu không có match tốt" (mục 2) chỉ áp dụng cho LỚP B-ROLL — không tự động kéo theo việc bỏ luôn caption của đoạn đó.

**Quy trình 2 bước (2026-07-17):** không phải mọi ý đều cần chèn B-roll — một số ý không cần. Thứ tự làm việc đúng là: (1) trước tiên xác định/liệt kê ý nào trong A-roll THỰC SỰ CẦN B-roll minh họa (không phải ý nào cũng cần), rồi (2) mới đi chọn/chèn clip cho từng ý đã xác định. Đừng nhảy thẳng vào chọn clip trước khi có danh sách ý cần chèn đã được xác nhận.

Rules confirmed by the user (2026-07-16) while building B-roll on the "Dựng video Rich Coenzyme q10" timeline in the `DiLiM_AI_Editor` Resolve project:

1. **Chọn B-roll theo Ý NGHĨA, không theo màu sắc hay hình ảnh.** When a reference/edited video uses e.g. an orange brain graphic for an idea, a blue or red brain graphic conveying the same concept is an acceptable substitute — don't hold out for exact color match. Applies project-wide, not just to this one video. **Re-emphasized strongly (2026-07-17, "khắc cốt ghi tâm"):** the unit of matching is ONE DISTINCT IDEA → ONE B-roll clip whose content matches that idea — e.g. "đau đầu" is its own idea/clip, "đau vai gáy" is a separate idea/clip, "mất ngủ" another, "tê bì chân tay" another, "rối loạn tiền đình" another, "hay quên" another — each gets its own single matching clip, not lumped together. Don't chase a reference video's rapid multi-clip montage style (many different people shown in quick succession) as if it were about visual variety — a rapid cut in a reference is not itself a spec to imitate unless the user explicitly asks for that editing style; the content-match-per-idea rule is the actual constant. (I misread this once: saw a reference clip cutting through ~5 different people in 5s and proposed rebuilding to add more per-idea clips for "dynamism" — user corrected this was the wrong read entirely.)
2. **Để trống nếu không có match tốt, đừng nhồi nhét.** If no B-roll clip in the library genuinely matches an idea, leave that stretch as plain A-roll rather than reusing/padding an unrelated or duplicate clip just to avoid a gap. (User explicitly had me revert 2 padding placements that existed only to fill time.)
3. **B-roll phải bắt đầu đúng tại từ khóa được nói, sớm hơn tối đa 1-3 khung hình** (at 60fps) — not aligned to whisper's sentence-level timestamps, which are too imprecise (can be off by seconds). Requires word-level timestamps (`faster_whisper` with `word_timestamps=True`), then mapping each word's source-audio time to the correct timeline frame via the per-clip linear offset (since the A-roll on the timeline is pre-cut into several disjoint source ranges).
4. **Thư mục `D:\download\Footage B-roll\Đã Chuẩn Hóa\`** is the user's own hand-renamed, idea-tagged B-roll pool — search here FIRST before any other library folder. File names are self-descriptive Vietnamese no-diacritic-ish slugs (e.g. `dau-dau-2,chong-mat,Roi-loan-tien-dinh.mp4`, `hay-quen,tri-nho-kem,nho-nho-quen-quen.mp4`). When searching by filename with Vietnamese diacritics, use NFC-normalize + lowercase comparison against `os.listdir()` output — direct hardcoded filenames risk NFC/NFD Unicode mismatches and case mismatches (e.g. `Não-thiếu-năng-lượng.mp4` has a capital Ñ) that silently fail `mp.ImportMedia()` even though `os.path.isfile()` may report true.
5. **Resolve's `AppendToTimeline` caps still-image and short-clip placements at ~300 frames** regardless of the requested `endFrame` — root cause unconfirmed. Workaround: place the same clip/image repeatedly back-to-back to cover a longer needed span.
6. **Preferred clip per idea, once user specifies one:** when the user names a specific file as their preferred clip for a given idea (e.g. "mất ngủ" → `D:\download\Footage B-roll\Đã Chuẩn Hóa\chong-mat-mat-ngu,thieu-ngu,mat-ngu-1.mp4`, stated 2026-07-17), treat that as the standing default for that idea in this and future videos, not just a one-off swap — use it first before other same-topic candidates in the library.
7. **Don't skip the opening hook/question by default — check its CONTENT, not its FORMAT.** Real miss (2026-07-21, video "dây thần kinh phế vị"): the opening line ("đau vai gáy... đầy bụng khó tiêu không?") was skipped for B-roll in the first pass because I pattern-matched "opening hook/question format" → "usually rhetorical, no B-roll needed" (a heuristic that correctly applies to genuinely content-free transition lines like "chỉ là một bệnh nhìn từ hai phía"). But this specific opening named two concrete, depictable symptoms — the same test ("can the viewer picture this?") should have caught it. Root cause: applied the skip-rule based on the sentence's POSITION/FORM (opening, phrased as a question) instead of re-checking its actual CONTENT. Fix: after the first "big-idea" pass, do a SECOND sentence-by-sentence sweep over the ENTIRE transcript — including the opening — asking only "does this sentence name a specific symptom/mechanism/cause?" regardless of where it sits or how it's phrased. Don't let a sentence's rhetorical shape excuse it from the content check.
8. **Imagine the ideal scene BEFORE searching, then verify against THAT specific scene, not against general topic relevance (2026-07-21).** Real miss (video "Tư thế ngồi làm việc", 17-27s, "cúi 15°: cổ chịu lực 12kg"): searched the literal transcript term "cột sống cổ" (cervical spine), got a glowing-nerve spine graphic, watched a frame of it, saw it was spine-related, accepted it — but the concept needed was MECHANICAL LOAD (weight/force from head-tilt angle), not nerve signaling; the clip only matched the general anatomical category, not the specific idea. Two-part fix: (a) before searching, articulate the ideal scene concretely — e.g. "person shown side-on, head tilted forward at increasing angle, looking at a screen/phone" — and derive search terms from THAT scene (tư thế cúi đầu, nhìn màn hình) rather than jumping straight to clinical terms lifted from the transcript; (b) — the part that actually prevents the error, per user's sharper follow-up: keyword refinement alone doesn't fix anything, since text search can always surface a same-category-wrong-concept result. The real fix is making the FRAME-VERIFICATION step ask the narrow question "does this clip depict the SPECIFIC scene/action I imagined?" instead of the loose question "is this clip thematically/topically related?" A clip that's merely adjacent-topic (same body part, same general subject) but doesn't depict the specific mechanism/action must be rejected, not accepted as "close enough" — fall back to a different search term, an empty gap, or (new capability, 2026-07-21) a simple hand-drawn PIL/SVG diagram instead.

9. **CẤM tự vẽ sơ đồ/hình minh họa thay B-roll, trừ khi tạo được ảnh 3D người thật (2026-07-21, standing hard rule).** Rule 8's earlier fallback list ("empty gap, hoặc simple hand-drawn PIL/SVG diagram") is now WRONG — the diagram option was tried once (2-panel PIL stick-figure for "tư thế thẳng 5kg vs cúi 15° 12kg" in "Tư thế ngồi làm việc") and the user rejected it outright, explicitly banning self-generated diagrams going forward: "từ giờ cấm bạn tự sinh sơ đồ trừ khi bạn tạo được ảnh 3d người thật". Claude has no photorealistic image-gen capability in this environment (no DALL-E/Midjourney/Imagen access), so in practice this is a TOTAL ban on drawn diagrams. When no real footage matches the specific concept: leave the segment as plain A-roll + animated caption text only (per rule 2 and the "nhịp dựng" text-alone principle above) — do not fabricate visuals of any kind as a stopgap.
10. **Verify B-roll clips at their TRUE rendered range, not an arbitrary point in the source file — and always check native-vs-needed duration first (2026-07-21).** Real bug found in "Tư thế ngồi làm việc": `render_overlay.py`'s `broll_chunk()` had no source-start-offset parameter at all — every clip always extracted frames starting at t=0 of the source file, regardless of where the relevant content actually sat inside a longer clip. This caused a phone-holding clip (26s long, phone scene at t=16-22s) to render its first ~6.6s instead — a stretching exercise, no phone visible — even though the FILE CHOICE was correct; only the in-file offset was wrong. This is systemic, not a one-off: fixed by adding a real `src_start_s` param (`-ss` before `-i`, with loop-if-short duration math adjusted accordingly), but the deeper lesson is procedural — (a) whenever a source file's native duration is much longer than the needed duration, treat it as high-risk for containing multiple different scenes and explicitly check WHERE in the file the relevant action occurs before trusting a single early verification frame; (b) after any placement, extract a verification frame at the clip's actual rendered start point (accounting for any offset), not t=0 or an arbitrary sample point — a single frame checked at the wrong offset can look "confirmed fine" while the real render shows something else entirely.

11. **Verify the MOOD/EXPRESSION of a clip, not just its surface subject — same food, opposite meaning (2026-07-22).** Real miss (video "Để sống khỏe thì làm gì", ~96s, caption "VẪN ĂN UỐNG HEALTHY HƠN"): picked `Đã Chuẩn Hóa/032 - Ngồi ăn món salad...` because it showed a woman eating salad — surface subject matched "ăn uống healthy". But the clip's own filename says "vẻ mặt chán ghét" (disgusted/reluctant expression) and the actual footage shows a bored, unhappy woman poking at food — the OPPOSITE emotional valence needed for an enthusiastic "eating healthy" message. It was actually the correct clip for a *different* idea: "chán ăn / ăn kiêng khắc nghiệt" (reluctant, forced dieting). Lesson: when a caption has an emotional valence (positive/enthusiastic vs. negative/reluctant), the frame-verification question from rule 8 must include mood/facial-expression, not just literal subject match — "person eating salad" is not one idea, it is at least two opposite ideas depending on how they look while doing it.
12. **User-specified preferred clip pools per recurring concept (2026-07-22, standing defaults — same weight as rule 6):**
    - **Ăn uống lành mạnh / healthy eating (positive, enthusiastic mood):** `D:\Download\Footage B-roll\Ăn uống lành mạnh\ăn-uong-lanh-manh.mp4`, `...\Ăn uống lành mạnh\Thiết kế chưa có tên (15).mp4`, `...\Ăn uống lành mạnh\8845448-uhd_4096_2160_24fps.mp4` — use these FIRST for any "ăn uống healthy/lành mạnh" idea, ahead of other same-topic candidates.
    - **Chán ăn / ăn kiêng khắc nghiệt / không ăn được rau (reluctant, forced, unhappy mood):** `D:\download\Footage B-roll\Giảm cân - Mập, tăng cân\033 - Dùng nĩa xúc rau xà lách ăn, vẻ mặt chán ghét khi ăn.mp4`, and `Đã Chuẩn Hóa\032 - Ngồi ăn món saladmì rau trộn bằng nĩa trong bếp, vẻ...mp4` (the clip corrected out of rule 11 above — its true home is THIS idea, not "healthy eating").
    - **Cơ thể khỏe mạnh, ẩn ý qua hình ảnh vận động (implied vitality, esp. across age):** `D:\download\Footage B-roll\Thể dục thể thao\Thiết kế chưa có tên - 2024-12-20T115808.557.mp4` (elderly couple jogging, smiling), `...\Thể dục thể thao\Thiết kế chưa có tên (3).mp4`, `...\Thể dục thể thao\Thiết kế chưa có tên - 2025-03-26T083741.007.mp4` — user's stated intent: images of people exercising serve as an implicit statement about a healthy body, prefer these over generic exercise footage when the point is "healthy body" itself rather than a literal "do exercise" instruction.

13. **KHÔNG lặp lại cùng 1 clip B-roll giống nhau trong 1 video, TRỪ B-roll sản phẩm (2026-07-23, standing hard rule).** Real miss (video "Sụn khớp không biết kêu"): reused `Xương khớp - Đau/084 - ...sụn tách rời...` twice (for "sụn có thể đang mòn ngay bây giờ" and again for "giai đoạn 1-2 sụn mòn dần") on the reasoning of rule 1 ("1 ý → 1 clip, ý lặp lại thì dùng lại clip đó") — but the user explicitly overrides that for non-product B-roll: even when the same idea recurs, find a DIFFERENT real clip from the library rather than reusing the identical footage, since the library (confirmed: 99 items in "Xương khớp - Đau" alone) has enough variety that repeats read as laziness/low effort, not fidelity to the idea. Product B-roll (official product photography/turntable video, e.g. `Product Broll/Nano Sụn cá mập Nhật Bản/Video/...`) is explicitly EXEMPT — reusing the same product shot across multiple product-mention captions is fine and expected, since there's usually only one official clip per product. **How to apply:** before finalizing a broll_plan, list every non-product clip path used and check for duplicates; if the same idea recurs, search again (or re-scan the already-listed folder contents from memory) for a second distinct real clip depicting that idea, rather than defaulting to reuse. This also means: don't just list a folder's contents and stop — actually open/verify multiple candidates per idea when the video has enough distinct beats to need variety, especially in content-rich folders the user specifically pointed to (e.g. "đa phần b-roll sẽ ở mục đau-xương khớp").

14. **Rebuild `broll_catalog.json` (`python catalog.py build`) at the START of every new job, before searching, not just when search comes up empty (2026-07-23).** Real miss (video "Sụn khớp không biết kêu"): `catalog.py search "nano sụn"` found nothing, so Claude concluded the product had no library footage — but the catalog JSON snapshot was from 2026-07-20, while the `Product Broll/GLUCHONGEL` folder had been added on 2026-07-22 (confirmed via folder mtime vs catalog file mtime). The catalog is a static snapshot, not a live index — any folder/file added after the last `build` is invisible to `search` even though it's on disk. A `Khong tim thay ket qua` result is therefore ambiguous (truly absent vs. stale index) unless the catalog was just rebuilt. **How to apply:** run `catalog.py build` as the first step of any new job before doing keyword searches, especially for product-specific footage the user recently supplied — cheap (a few seconds), and removes an entire class of false "the library doesn't have this" conclusions. If a search comes up empty mid-session after already building once, rebuild again before trusting the negative result, since the user may add files between turns.

15. **If the video mentions a product by name, its B-roll MUST come from `D:\download\Footage B-roll\Product Broll` — the CORRECT matching product only, never a substitute (2026-07-23, standing hard rule).** When A-roll names/pitches a specific product (e.g., "Nano Sụn Premium", "Gluchongel"), do not illustrate it with a different DiLiM product's footage (e.g., don't use Rich Coenzyme Q10 photography for a video about Nano Sụn) even if no exact-match folder seems to exist at first glance — rebuild the catalog (rule 14) and search again before concluding a product has no dedicated folder. Each product folder under `Product Broll/<Product Name>/` typically contains `Video/` (official turntable/photography), `Nguyên Liệu/` (per-ingredient assets, sometimes branded bubble-callout clips), and `Ảnh/` (stills); check all three subfolders, not just `Video/`.
16. **Each product is paired with its own topic-specific B-roll pool elsewhere in `D:\download\Footage B-roll\`, prearranged by the user (2026-07-23).** E.g., Nano Sụn Cá Mập/Gluchongel (joint/cartilage) pairs with the `Xương khớp - Đau` folder; other products likely pair similarly with their own symptom/topic folder (Rich Coenzyme Q10 ↔ heart/energy-related folders, Hàu Nano Gold ↔ its relevant topic, etc. — confirm the pairing for a given product with the user if not obvious from the product's stated purpose). When a job's A-roll is a product video, identify the product's paired topic folder FIRST (from the product's stated health purpose) and search there for the symptom/mechanism B-roll, in addition to `Product Broll/<Product>/` for the product-shot B-roll itself.

19. **Frame-count bug in the render pipeline: `-t` computed as `n/fps` and formatted to 4 dp silently drops 1 frame, and the loss ACCUMULATES across chunks (2026-07-29).** Found while verifying the "Sụn khớp không đau khi đang mòn" build: `01_BROLL.mov` had the correct 8486 frames but `02_TEXT.mov` had 8474 — 12 short. Per-chunk audit showed exactly 12 caption chunks each 1 frame short. Root cause in `render_overlay.py`: both `transparent_chunk()` and `caption_chunk()` passed `-t f"{n_frames/fps:.4f}"` to a `lavfi` source, then `-frames:v n_frames`. For any n where n/fps has a repeating decimal (188/60 = 3.13333… → "3.1333" → 187.998 frames) the source ends one frame early and `-frames:v` cannot add a frame back. Fixed by `_t_arg(n, fps) = (n + 2)/fps` — give `-t` headroom and let `-frames:v` do the exact trim; verified on 10 problem values, all now exact. **Why this matters beyond the missing frames:** the track is assembled by frame-exact concat, so every short chunk shifts EVERYTHING after it one frame earlier — a cumulative drift that pulls later captions ahead of the spoken word. This is very likely a contributing cause of the user's earlier complaint on the Nano Sụn build ("có nhiều chỗ text xuất hiện trước hoặc sau lời nói trong suốt video"), which at the time I attributed only to caption boundary choices. Standing check to run on every render before delivery: `ffprobe` each output layer and assert `nb_frames == meta.total_frames`; if short, audit chunk-by-chunk rather than assuming the plan was wrong. Note the bug hits `-t` on synthetic/looped sources only — `broll_chunk()` uses `-frames:v` alone and was never affected, which is why the B-roll layer stayed correct and the discrepancy only showed on the text layer.
